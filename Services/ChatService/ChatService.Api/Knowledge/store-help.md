# Orders and tracking

## How do I track my order?
Sign in and open **Orders** from the menu to see all your orders. Each order has a public order number in the form ORD-XXXXXXXX (for example ORD-1A2B3C4D). Select an order to see its status, items and total. The chat assistant can also look up an order for you once you are signed in; give it the order number.

## What do the order statuses mean?
- **Pending**: the order has been placed and is waiting to be confirmed.
- **Confirmed**: the order was confirmed (this happens after payment succeeds).
- **Processing**: the order is being prepared.
- **Shipped**: the order has left the store.
- **Delivered**: the order has been delivered.
- **Cancelled**: the order was cancelled.
The store does not show carrier names, tracking links or delivery dates. If you need those, contact support.

## Can I cancel my order?
You can cancel an order only while its status is **Pending**. Open the order from **Orders** and choose cancel. Once an order is Confirmed or later it can no longer be cancelled online; contact support for help.

## I cannot find my order
Check that you are signed in with the account used to place the order. Orders are only visible to the account that placed them. If it is still missing, contact support with the order number or the approximate date.

## I get a notification about my order
Signed-in customers see a bell icon with order updates (placed, cancelled, status changes). Select a notification to open the order.

# Checkout and payment

## How do I place an order?
Add products to the cart, open **Cart**, then **Checkout**. Choose or add a delivery address, then place the order and complete payment. You must be signed in to check out.

## What happens if payment fails or I close the page?
The order stays **Pending** with an unpaid status and you can return to checkout to pay for it again. Pending orders that you no longer want can be cancelled from **Orders**.

## Which payment methods are accepted?
The payment methods shown on the checkout page are the ones available. The assistant does not have a separate list of payment methods. Never share card numbers, CVV or passwords in chat; the assistant cannot take payments.

## Why does the price at checkout differ from what I expected?
Prices are set by the store at the time you place the order, not by the browser. The cart and checkout pages always show the current price. Prices can change between visits.

# Account

## How do I reset my password?
On the sign-in page choose **Forgot password**, enter your email, then enter the one-time code (OTP) sent to you and choose a new password. If no code arrives, check your spam folder and try again.

## How do I verify my email?
After signing up you receive a one-time code by email. Enter it on the **Verify your email** page.

## How do I add or change my delivery address?
Open **My account** (profile) and use the address book to add, edit or remove addresses. Your saved address is used at checkout.

## How do I change my name or contact details?
Open **My account** and edit your details there.

# Products

## How do I find a product?
Use the search box or browse **Products**. You can filter by category and price and sort by name or price.

## Do products have reviews or ratings?
The store does not currently show customer ratings or reviews, so the assistant cannot compare products by rating.

## Is a product in stock?
Availability is shown on the product page and at checkout. The assistant cannot see live stock levels.

# Help

## How do I contact support?
Use the contact details on the **Contact** page. For anything the assistant cannot answer, such as refunds, damaged items, delivery issues or account problems, ask for a human agent.

## Returns, refunds, shipping fees and delivery times
Details about returns, refunds, shipping fees and delivery times are not available here. Please use the Contact page and a person will help. The AI assistant must not guess these policies.

## Is the assistant a real person?
No. It is an AI assistant. It can make mistakes, so check important details such as order status on the Orders page. You can always ask for a human.
